# AbacSoft

**AI Teams, Not Just Agents.**

AbacSoft is building AI-powered teams that work together like real departments.

Instead of relying on a single AI agent to handle everything, AbacSoft brings together specialized agents — researchers, writers, engineers, reviewers, analysts, and more — and orchestrates them through structured, observable workflows.

Give your AI team a goal, and it handles the rest: planning, research, execution, review, and delivery.

### What We're Building

- ✍️ **Writing Team** — Research, writing, editing, fact-checking, and publishing
- 📰 **Newsroom Team** — News discovery, verification, analysis, and reporting
- 👨‍💻 **Software Team** — Requirements, architecture, development, testing, and code review
- 📈 **Marketing Team** — Market research, SEO, content, campaigns, and publishing

### Our Philosophy

We believe the future of AI is not a single assistant that does everything.

It's a **team of specialized AI experts** that collaborate, review each other's work, and operate through transparent and repeatable workflows.

Every step should be observable.  
Every result should be auditable.  
Every workflow should be customizable.
